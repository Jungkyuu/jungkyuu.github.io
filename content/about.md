## About Me

Hi, I'm **Jung Kyu Mok**. I'm a Computer Science (Honours BSc, Co-op) student at the **University of Ottawa**, and I graduated from the Computer Programming and Analysis program at **Seneca Polytechnic**.

I like building full-stack web apps, automating cloud setups, and making software that works reliably. I've worked with web development, cloud deployment, databases, and Linux, and I enjoy taking a problem from start to finish.

Here's how I got here:
- **Military Leadership:** Before moving to Canada, I served in the Republic of Korea Army as a Tactical Communications Operator and Squad Leader. I learned to work in a team, lead others, and fix problems quickly under pressure.
- **Hands-on Experience (Seneca Polytechnic):** I learned C, C++, JavaScript, and SQL, worked with teammates on group projects, and launched a full web application for my capstone project.
- **Theory (University of Ottawa):** I'm now studying algorithms, math, and system design to connect what I've built in practice with the core ideas of computer science.
